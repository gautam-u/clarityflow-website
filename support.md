## Getting Started

### How do I record a decision?

Tap the **+** button and enter what you're considering, along with the cost. ClarityFlow captures it and starts a cooling-off period so you can decide with a clear head instead of in the moment.

### What is the cooling-off timer?

A deliberate waiting period — 24 hours, 48 hours, or a custom duration — before you commit to a purchase. Most impulse buys lose their pull after a short wait. When the timer ends, ClarityFlow asks whether you still want it. Either answer is recorded, so you build a picture of your own patterns over time.

### What is AI Chat Import?

If you've talked through a purchase with an AI assistant, paste that conversation into ClarityFlow. It extracts the decision title, the cost, and the billing interval automatically, so you don't have to retype anything.

### How does the subscription tracker work?

Add each recurring charge with its amount and billing interval — monthly, annual, or weekly. ClarityFlow totals your monthly burn and reminds you before renewals, which is usually the last chance to cancel something you no longer use.

## Premium

### What is included in Pro?

Pro unlocks the detailed scorecard, spending-pattern analysis, and the full decision library.

### How much does Pro cost?

$2.99 per month, or $19.99 per year.

### How do I subscribe?

Open ClarityFlow → Settings → Upgrade. Purchases are handled entirely through Apple's App Store.

### How do I cancel?

iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ClarityFlow → Cancel Subscription. Access continues until the end of the billing period you've already paid for.

### I paid but Pro is not active

Confirm you're signed into the same Apple ID used for the purchase, then open ClarityFlow → Settings → Upgrade to Premium → **Restore Purchases**. If that doesn't resolve it, email us and we'll sort it out.

## Your Data

### Where is my data stored?

On your device. ClarityFlow does not upload your decisions to any server, and no account is required. Full detail is in the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy-policy' | relative_url }}).

### Can I export my decisions?

Yes — Settings → Export Data.

### How do I delete everything?

Deleting the app removes all local data permanently. There is no server-side copy to clear.

### Will I lose my data if I change phones?

Data lives on the device. Restoring from an encrypted iPhone backup carries it across. Export first if you're moving devices without a backup.

## Troubleshooting

### My cooling-off reminder never arrived

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → ClarityFlow and confirm notifications are allowed. Focus modes and Low Power Mode can also delay delivery.

### A subscription renewal reminder was wrong or missing

Reminders are generated from the billing interval and start date you entered. Open the subscription and confirm both are correct — a wrong start date shifts every future reminder.

### AI Chat Import didn't pick up the details

Extraction works best when the pasted text states the item and a clear price. If a field comes through blank or wrong, you can edit it before saving.
