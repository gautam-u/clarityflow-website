How can we help?

Contact gautam.ux@gmail.com with the app version, device model, iOS version and a short description of what happened. Please leave out private records, payment details and anything you do not want to share.

Common questions

Does ClarityFlow connect to my bank?

No. Summaries reflect the records you add. An expected subscription renewal is not proof that a payment happened.

Does skipping a purchase count as money saved?

A purchase you decide to skip is a recorded decision, not verified cash savings. It is kept separate from actual expenses.

Can I use ordinary expense fields?

Yes. Manual entry remains available. Optional text interpretation requires a supported Apple Intelligence device with an available model, and you review the draft before saving.

Purchases and subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Manage or cancel an auto-renewing subscription in your Apple Account settings. The App Store handles purchases and refund requests.
